FEATURES

Built for the whole day

◔

Real-time bars

Willpower and Wealth start full and reach zero on your schedule — default 05:00–22:00, with overnight and custom windows. Back/swipe returns to the previous tab.

◎

Activity tracking

Step counter, walking distance from steps, active minutes and the last seven days. An hourly activity timeline shows when you walked, with last-10-min, top and low walking speed estimated from step cadence.

◒

Energy follows you

Steps and movement drain Energy Power. Rest and detected sleep restore it automatically.

▤

Four widgets

2×2, 4×1, 4×2 and 4×4 home-screen widgets that follow your theme and activity.

◈

Sixteen calm themes

Midnight, Nord, Solarized Light, Gruvbox, Rosé Pine, Catppuccin Mocha, Tokyo Night and more. Applied instantly everywhere.

⌁

History & chart

Local SQLite history with a 7-day average, 14-day chart and CSV export including steps.

⚑

Threshold alerts

Choose 75 / 50 / 25 / 10% per power. One consolidated alert per crossing.

⛨

Fully offline

No account, no analytics, no INTERNET permission. Everything stays on device.

Get Vitality

Download the signed APK and install it on any Android 10+ phone. Activity access is requested once; the power schedule, themes and tracking live in Settings.
